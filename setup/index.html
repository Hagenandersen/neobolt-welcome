02

Log in to your CRM and get the app

This is where every call, text, form and message lands. Reply from the truck.

About 5 min
  1. Find the email with your login. It comes from LeadConnector or HighLevel. Check spam too. Tap the link and set your password.
  2. No email? Go to the login page, enter your email, tap Forgot password.
    Open the login page
  3. Install the LeadConnector app on your phone.
    iPhone Android
  4. Log in with the same email. If it asks for a location, pick your business.
  5. When your phone asks, allow notifications and the microphone. Without them you won't hear new leads.

Steps 3 to 8 are easiest on a computer, logged in at the same login page.

03

Connect your social media

So we can post for you and every message lands in one inbox. You stay the owner.

About 5 min

In your CRM, on the left menu: Marketing › Social Planner.

Open Social Planner
  1. Click + Connect under Facebook. Log in, pick your business Page, and allow everything it asks.
  2. Click + Connect under Instagram. It must be a business or creator account linked to your Facebook Page. Instagram app: Settings › Account type and tools to switch, it's free.
  3. Click + Connect under GBP (your Google Business Profile). Log in with the Google account that owns your profile and pick your business.
  4. Have LinkedIn, TikTok or YouTube for the business? Connect them the same way. No? Skip them.
04

Get your business phone number

A local number for your website, Google and ads, so every call and text is tracked. Missed calls can still ring your cell.

About 10 min

In your CRM: Settings (bottom left) › Phone System › Phone Numbers tab.

Open Phone Numbers
  1. Click the blue + Add Number button (top right).
  2. Search by your area code and pick a local number. Not toll-free.
  3. It will check that it's really you. It asks for a quick face scan with your phone camera and a photo of your driver's license or ID. That's normal. It's the phone company's identity check, and Neobolt never sees your ID.
  4. Finish the purchase. If it asks for a payment card and you're not sure, stop and text Hagen first.
STOP at the texting registration.

After the number, it may ask you to register for texting (it says A2P, 10DLC or Trust Center). Do not fill it in or submit it. Close that screen. Hagen files it for you. A mistake there gets your texts blocked for weeks.

05

Connect Google

Your email and calendar, so booked jobs show on your calendar. And your Google profile, so reviews and messages come in.

About 5 min

In your CRM: Settings › Integrations.

Open Integrations
  1. Find Google and click Connect. Log in with your business Gmail. Allow Gmail and Calendar.
  2. On the same page, find Google Business Profile and click Connect. Use the Google account that owns your profile, and pick your business.
  3. Already connected Google in Social Planner (step 3)? Still do it here too. They're two separate switches.
06

Connect Facebook and Instagram

So Facebook and Instagram messages and lead forms come straight into your CRM.

About 3 min

In your CRM: Settings › Integrations.

Open Integrations
  1. Find Facebook & Instagram and click Connect.
  2. Log in to Facebook. When it asks, pick your business Page and your Instagram account, and leave every permission switched on.
  3. Back in the CRM, choose your Page from the list and save.
You don't need to give us Meta Business Manager access. This connection is all we need. Nothing else to set up on Facebook.
07

Add Hagen as a Manager on Google

So we can fix your Google profile, post every week and answer reviews. You stay the owner, and you can remove us any time.

About 2 min
Open Google Business Profile
  1. Sign in with the Google account that owns your profile. Pick your business.
  2. Click the ⋮ (three dots) menu, then Business Profile settings.
  3. Click People and access (older screens say Managers), then Add.
  4. Type hagen@neoboltsystems.com
  5. Set access to Manager (not Owner), then Invite.

On your phone instead: Google Maps app, tap your profile photo, Your Business Profiles, your business, three dots, Business Profile settings, People and access.
