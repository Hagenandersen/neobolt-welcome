  2. In the box that says Your business on Google, click the three-dot menu, then Business Profile settings.

On your phone instead: Google Maps app, tap your profile photo, Your Business Profiles, your business, three-dot menu, Business Profile settings, People and access.

  4. Paste our Business ID 1003510354042185

05

What happens next

Here's the first 30 days. You'll hear from Hagen on day 7 and day 14, and get your first report on day 30.

  1. Day 0You finish steps 1 to 4

    We write down your starting numbers: calls, reviews, jobs a month and where they come from today. Every result gets measured against this.

  2. Days 1 to 3Your system gets built

    Your CRM and app, your tracked local number, and your texting registration filed with the phone carriers. Google profile cleaned up.

  3. Days 2 to 7Fixes go live

    Quote form tested and fixed, online booking added, call forwarding set up and tested with you. First posts drafted for your OK.

  4. Week 2Automations switch on

    Review requests after every job, instant replies to forms and messages, quote follow-ups. Text messages start once the carriers approve.

  5. Week 3Local SEO

    Pages for your top towns, and your listings matched everywhere so Google trusts them.

  6. Day 30Your first report

    Everything tested end to end, and a plain-English report against your day-0 numbers.

  7. Every monthNew-jobs review

    A short call on the day you picked. We go through the month's jobs together, each with where it came from, and agree which ones are new jobs we brought you. Your regulars and referrals stay yours.

  8. Day 90Your 90-day review

    We count the new jobs that came through what we run, against your day-0 numbers, and plan the next 90 days.

About texting. Missed-call text-back and review texts start when the phone carriers approve your business. That usually takes a few business days after we file, sometimes longer. Until then you get an app alert for every missed call, so nothing slips.

What we need from you after today

  • Job photos as you take them. Phone pictures are perfect. Before and after is gold.
  • A quick OK on your first posts and on how we reply to reviews.
  • Move a job to Job Completed in the app when it's done. That's what sends the review request.
  • Mark where each job came from when you log it. One word is enough.

What we never do

  • Message your customers, or post as you, without your OK first.
  • Take ownership of anything. Your Google profile, website, social accounts and customer list stay yours.
  • Share or sell your logins or customer data. They're used only to run your marketing.
